About

A data scientist who teaches, and a teacher who ships. For over a decade I've turned complex AI and data concepts into skills people use at work the next day.

I work across Generative AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, Data Science and Analytics. As a Senior Data Scientist at PNT Robotics I've built transformer-based NLP and Visual Question Answering systems and taken 7+ projects from prototype to production.

As a trainer and curriculum designer, I've built certification programs for Simplilearn and Emeritus, taught 40+ batches end-to-end at Imarticus and ITVedant, and delivered 25+ programs as an IBM Innovation Centre for Education professional.

I also shape what gets taught: I sit on the Board of Studies at seven institutions and the Board of Examination at TransStadia Institute.
